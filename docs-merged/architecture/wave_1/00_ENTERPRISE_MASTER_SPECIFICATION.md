## What this document is

The index for the Product tree, mirroring P-00's role in the Protocol tree exactly: what exists, what wave it belongs to, whether it's safe to build against.

## What the company sells, restated once here

Not the protocol — that's free, permanently (P-38). Continuous operation, accumulated intelligence, and accountability: hosted infrastructure a customer doesn't have to run, fraud signal no single customer can generate alone, compliance packaging a customer would otherwise build in-house, and a real party to call when something breaks.

## Reading this table honestly

Wave 4 is where this dashboard stops being reassuring. Eight documents, all Priority P0, and five of them read **Open** — not "in progress," open by design, because the underlying question can't be answered by more writing. That split is the single most important thing this table shows, and it's shown here deliberately rather than smoothed over to make the dashboard look more finished.
