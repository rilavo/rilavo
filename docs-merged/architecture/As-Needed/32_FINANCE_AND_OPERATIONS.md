# Rilavo Product — Finance & Operations (E-32)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** Continuous / As-Needed
**Status:** Deferred
**Depends on:** None
**Closes when:** Revenue requires dedicated financial operations beyond what a solo founder can track directly — not on a fixed timeline.

## Why this document doesn't invent process the company doesn't have

A pre-revenue, solo-founder company has no finance function to describe honestly beyond "the founder tracks it." Writing a detailed financial-operations policy now would be performing organizational maturity that doesn't exist — precisely the failure mode this entire project has avoided everywhere else, and no more acceptable here for being a less technical document than the rest.

## What's flagged for later, not decided now

Revenue recognition policy once real revenue exists (E-17). Expense management once spend crosses a threshold worth formal tracking. Financial reporting cadence once there are real stakeholders — investors, a board (E-35) — who need it.

## What would change this decision

Real revenue or real external stakeholders making informal tracking genuinely insufficient — a concrete, observable threshold, not a date chosen in advance.
