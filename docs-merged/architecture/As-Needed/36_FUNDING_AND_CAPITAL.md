# Rilavo Product — Funding & Capital (E-36)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** Continuous / As-Needed
**Status:** Deferred
**Depends on:** None
**Closes when:** A real capital decision is actually made — not flagged in advance of one existing.

## Why this document is not the same question as E-40

E-40 concerns whether Product's *own money*, once it has revenue, should be able to buy governance power over the *protocol* — already decided there: no. This document concerns something entirely separate: whether Product itself raises outside capital, from whom, and on what terms. Confusing the two would blur a distinction worth keeping sharp — funding the company and funding the protocol are different questions with different stakes.

## What's flagged for later, not decided now

Whether outside capital is raised at all, or whether the business is built to be sustainable on revenue alone. If capital is raised: what governance rights investors receive over the *company* (E-35) — explicitly not over the *protocol* (P-24), which E-40's structure is built specifically to keep insulated from exactly this kind of pressure regardless of where Product's own funding comes from.

## The one thing this document commits to now, even while deferring the rest

Whatever funding decision Product eventually makes, it cannot be structured in a way that creates leverage over protocol governance — a condition already guaranteed by E-40's own architecture, restated here so a future funding decision is evaluated against it explicitly rather than discovered to conflict with it after the fact.

## What would change this decision

An actual funding conversation becoming real — at which point this document records the terms considered and chosen, checked against the one constraint above before anything is finalized, not after.
