# Rilavo Product — Security Services (E-11)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** Continuous / As-Needed
**Status:** Deferred
**Depends on:** E-07
**Closes when:** Real, repeated demand for a standalone product appears — not before.

## What this would be, if it existed

Proactive, paid help configuring verification correctly — a secure-integration review distinct from reactive incident response (E-13) and distinct from the pattern-level fraud intelligence feed (E-09). At v0, this folds entirely into ordinary developer-platform support (E-07).

## Why it stays folded in rather than becoming its own line item

Unbundling a product before there's real, repeated demand for it creates pricing and sales overhead against a guess. Nothing about this need is urgent enough at v0's scale to justify that overhead yet.

## The specific signal that would change this

Not a single customer asking for help once — that's ordinary support. The signal is a *pattern*: multiple customers independently requesting the same kind of proactive review, especially if they're willing to pay specifically for it rather than expecting it bundled into their existing tier. That pattern, once observed, is the actual trigger — not a roadmap date.

## What would change this decision

The pattern described above, observed in real customer requests over a defined window — at which point this document graduates from a placeholder to an actual product specification, informed by what customers were specifically asking for rather than a guess at what they might want.
