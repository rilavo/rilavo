# Rilavo Product — Marketing (E-23)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** Continuous / As-Needed
**Status:** Deferred
**Depends on:** E-19
**Closes when:** Product-led growth data says a dedicated function is needed — not before.

## Why this stays deferred

Documentation quality, integration guides, and transparent public incident writeups already do more work for this audience than campaign marketing would (E-18's own reasoning, restated here rather than re-argued). Standing up a marketing function before there's evidence the product-led motion has plateaued would be spending against a problem that hasn't been shown to exist yet.

## What already functions as marketing, without being labeled that way

The documentation itself. Public postmortems (E-13). The developer-relations function (E-24) — all doing distribution work already, which is exactly why a separate marketing budget isn't an obvious next step just because the company is growing.

## The signal that would change this

Growth from the product-led and network-distribution loops (E-18, E-19) plateauing while real, addressable demand is still visible elsewhere — a specific, checkable condition, not a vague sense that "marketing would probably help."

## What would change this decision

That plateau being observed for real, with evidence that the bottleneck is discovery rather than product fit or sales process — at which point this document becomes a real function specification rather than a placeholder.
