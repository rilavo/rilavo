# Rilavo Product — Decision Log (E-45)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** 5 — Governance & Institutionalization
**Status:** Decided (practice), ongoing
**Depends on:** Every document in the Product tree
**Closes when:** Never — same as P-44, for the same reason.

## Format

Identical to P-44's specification: Decision ID, Date, Status, Decision, Rationale, What would change it. One log format across both trees, deliberately, rather than two formats that could quietly diverge.

## Why a separate file from P-44, given the identical format

Because the two trees' decisions have different owners and different review cadences — Product's economics and commercial decisions (E-14 through E-21) get revisited against market and cost data on a different rhythm than Protocol's cryptographic and architectural decisions (P-06 through P-22) do. Keeping the logs separate, sharing only the format, lets each tree's log stay current on its own schedule without one tree's faster-moving decisions burying the other's slower, more structural ones.

## What already exists

Every **Decision** and **Rejected** line across all fourteen Wave 3 documents, all eight Wave 4 documents, and this wave's eight Product documents already constitutes this log's raw content — not yet compiled into one running chronological file, the same honest gap named in P-44.

## What would change this decision

Nothing about the format, which is intentionally identical to P-44's. The compiled log's content changes constantly, by design, as its source documents' statuses do.
