# Rilavo Product — Corporate Governance (E-35)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** 5 — Governance & Institutionalization
**Status:** Deferred, deliberately kept light
**Depends on:** E-27
**Closes when:** The company's actual stage warrants more structure than exists today — not on a fixed timeline.

## The one point this document actually decides

Corporate governance — the legal entity's own board, decision rights, and structure — must stay institutionally separate from protocol governance (P-24). Conflating the two is exactly the capture risk named in E-27: a company whose own board doubles as the protocol's governance body has quietly made the "neutral protocol" claim false in substance, regardless of what any document says about it.

## Why this document stops there, on purpose

Inventing a detailed board structure, voting rights, or officer roles for a solo-founder, pre-revenue company would be exactly the kind of manufactured institutional weight this entire project has avoided everywhere else — a document performing more organizational maturity than actually exists yet. **This is deliberate**, not an oversight matching the shallower treatment this wave gave its genuinely-gated Protocol documents (P-16, P-21, P-33): those are gated on external triggers outside Rilavo's control; this one is gated on internal company growth, which is just as real a gate even though it's self-imposed rather than externally forced.

## What this document is honest about not being

Not a claim that corporate governance doesn't matter — it will, once there's a board to govern, employees whose reporting lines need defining, and investors or stakeholders with real decision rights to structure. None of that exists yet, and describing it as though it does would misrepresent the company's actual current shape.

## What would change this document

Real growth — a first hire beyond the founder, an investment requiring board representation, or any other concrete event that makes "the founder decides everything, informally" no longer an accurate description of how the company actually operates. At that point, this document earns the same depth Wave 5's other governance documents received.
