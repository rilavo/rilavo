# Rilavo Product — Protocol Funding & Stewardship (E-40)

**Tree:** Product
**Wave:** 5 — Governance & Institutionalization
**Status:** Decided (principle, and now a concrete structural proposal below); Open (whether the proposal survives contact with real funding flows)
**Depends on:** P-38, P-24
**Closes when:** Product actually funds something at real scale — the proposal below is designed in advance of that moment specifically so the structure isn't invented under pressure once real money is involved.

## The principle, unchanged

No money enters the protocol layer as a matter of course (P-38). As Product matures, it will plausibly become the largest funder of governance activity, audits, or ecosystem grants around the protocol — and governance seats must never become proportional to funding contributed, regardless of how large that funding grows.

## A concrete structural proposal, not left abstract

**Funding flows through a dedicated fund structure, modeled on how some open-source foundations accept corporate sponsorship without attaching board seats to donation size** — contribution buys recognition and, at most, a non-voting advisory presence, never a governance vote proportional to the amount given. The governance body specified in P-24 — issuer seats, operator seats, the independent public-interest seat — remains entirely unaffected by who funds the fund.

## Worked example

Product funds a security audit of the reference implementation, a real and legitimate expense benefiting the whole network. Under this structure, that funding doesn't earn Product an additional governance vote, doesn't give it veto power over the audit's findings, and doesn't let it select which auditor runs the review unilaterally — the fund structure exists specifically to keep "who pays" and "who decides" as separate questions, even when it would be operationally convenient to let them merge.

## Why this is proposed now rather than designed later

Designing funding governance *after* Product is already the dominant funder would be designing it under exactly the pressure this document exists to avoid — at that point, every design choice would carry the appearance of being shaped by whoever already holds the money. Proposing the structure now, before any real funding flow exists, means the principle gets tested against a design made in the absence of that pressure.

## What this document is honest about not knowing

Whether this specific structure survives contact with real funding flows and real governance disputes, or whether a genuine test case reveals a gap the proposal didn't anticipate — normal for any first-draft institutional design, and not treated here as more settled than that.

## What would change this decision

The first real funding decision at meaningful scale — which either validates this structure or reveals its first real flaw, informing a revision grounded in an actual case rather than continued speculation.
